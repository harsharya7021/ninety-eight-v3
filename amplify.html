02 The room What we actually do when a brief says “influencer marketing”

One creator per audience never five from one shelf

Room 02 — The Noise

This is the second of Marketing’s five rooms and the one most of the archive lives in. A brief usually arrives asking for influencers. What it needs is a stack: one verified voice per audience class — founder, fashion-travel, couples-food, fashion-digital — landing inside a window tight enough that a city reads it as an arrival rather than a schedule. Five creators from the same shelf is one creator, bought five times.

The counting is done the same way every time, because that is the only thing that makes the numbers comparable: creators activated, verified handles, story frames captured, combined follower reach. Highlighted, confirmed and pending are counted separately rather than inflated together — Andrea’s Eatery is the reference case for that. Reach is combined follower reach, not impressions, and we say so.

PR and media sit in this room too. They are the earned half of the same job: a creator makes the city talk, a journalist makes the record. The launch that gets written about is in room 01; the noise that follows it is here.
