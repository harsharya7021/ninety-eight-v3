02 The room What Retain is, and the eight cases filed under it

The feed is held week after week

The room:

Retain is the fourth of Marketing’s five rooms — Launch, Amplify, Convert, Retain, Events — and the one the studio lives in. The channels we build for a brand are held here week after week: the feed, the design system, the shoots. Three kinds of work, three parts to this page.

The feed is above — social creatives, stories and posts for eleven brands. The identity work is at the foot of the page. The shoots have a page of their own, because a shoot is a channel we own, not a service sold apart from it.
