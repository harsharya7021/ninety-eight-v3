Production, in-house:

Production lives in Retain, the room where the channels we build for a brand are held week after week. A shoot is one of those channels — the menus, the ranges, the rooms and the people that the feed is made of — and it is run here, not bought in.

Fourteen of these were shot for food and hospitality houses with Chitra by RK behind the camera; the last two are from the talent practice’s floor. The reel on the talent page cuts the floor to nineteen seconds.
