What thirty-five cases taught us

2020—2023 · 70+ brands · ten cities

Patterns observed, not principles claimed. Patterns are evidence; principles are marketing.

01

The constellation

A stack of creators across audience classes inside a tight window. Slay (58), One8 Commune (36 creators, 98 frames), Sky Lantern, Farzi Cafe, Deja Brew.

02

The single-creator anchor

One verified voice whose feed already lives in the brand’s grammar. Bayroute, Ophelia.

03

The daypart play

Sell the hour nobody else is selling. Khubani’s 1–6 PM spa lunch, The Weekend’s Rock ’n’ Brunch.

04

One creator per audience class

Founder, fashion-travel, couples-food, fashion-digital — never five from the same shelf.

05

One retainer, many voices

A shared creator pool across sister brands. Yum Yum Cha Group.

06

The founder cameo

A credibility lift from founder-class creators. Khubani, Farzi Cafe.

07

Three tiers, not one headline

Highlighted, confirmed, pending — counted separately instead of inflated together. Andrea’s Eatery is the reference case.
