02 What this is And what it isn’t
The rule:

Everything here comes out of work we did and can point at. Where a piece uses a number, the case that earned it is one click away in the archive; where there is no case yet, the piece says so rather than reaching for a principle. That is the same rule the rest of this site runs on.

It is not a content calendar. There is no schedule, and nothing is published to be published. A piece appears when we have learned something across enough clients that it stops being an anecdote and starts being a pattern.
