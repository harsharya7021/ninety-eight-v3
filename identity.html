02 The room Why identity comes first, and the launches it fed

Identity is the first room not the last deliverable

Room 01 — The Opening

Marketing at Ninety-Eight is five rooms, and this is the first of them. Before a brand can be launched, amplified, converted, retained or thrown a party, somebody has to decide what it means — the name, the position, the mark, the lockups, the type, the colour, the system that has to survive a feed it was never designed for.

We do that work, and then we open the doors with it. The identity is not a deck that gets signed off and filed; it is the thing the press cycle, the creator brief and the menu photograph all have to agree with six months later. Seven pieces are below — a bar identity and its visual system, two directions for one burger brand, a jeweller’s mark and reveal film, a brewery banner, and three product concepts we designed for the practice of it. The first three open a showcase of their own.

Four cases in the archive were opened with work from this room. They are listed underneath.
